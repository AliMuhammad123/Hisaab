Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [COUNTRY].

What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored only as a one-way scrambled value), your country and currency, and the records you enter: customers, credit given, payments, monthly plans and personal entries.

For sign-in security we keep a short log of failed sign-in attempts with the IP address, for about a day.

What we do with it

We use it only to run the app for you: to show your balances, check your limits and work out your forecast. The forecast is calculated on our own server from your entries. Nothing is sent to an outside AI service.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools. The app loads no files from other companies.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in. Other CreditFlow users cannot see your records. The people who run the server could technically read the database, and do so only when needed to fix a problem.

Your choices

In Settings you can download all of your data and delete your account. Deleting your account removes every record in it from our database straight away.
