Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [COUNTRY].

What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored only as a one-way scrambled value), your country and currency, and the records you enter: customers, credit given, payments, monthly plans and personal entries.

Your records are stored with Supabase, our database provider, which also handles sign-in and keeps short-term security logs such as sign-in attempts and IP addresses.

What we do with it

We use it only to run the app for you: to show your balances, check your limits and work out your forecast. The forecast is calculated on your phone from your entries. Nothing is sent to an outside AI service.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools. The app contains no tracking or advertising code.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in. Other CreditFlow users cannot see your records: the database itself refuses to show anyone rows that are not theirs. The people who run CreditFlow and our database provider could technically read the database, and do so only when needed to fix a problem.

Your choices

In Settings you can delete your account. To get a copy of your data, email us. Deleting your account removes every record in it from our database straight away.
